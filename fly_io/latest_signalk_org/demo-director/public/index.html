Take the helm

The demo boat sails its route on autopilot. Log in and you steer it: the demo login may change the autopilot and nothing else.

Login demo, password demo.

  1. Open Freeboard-SK and its autopilot console.
  2. Pick a mode, engage, and steer with ±1° and ±10°.
  3. Leave the autopilot alone for five minutes and the boat goes back to its route. Other visitors may be steering too.
